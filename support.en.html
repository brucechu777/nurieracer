Support & FAQ

Written for parents and guardians.

How to print the coloring sheet

Choosing "Color on paper" in the App creates a PDF of the car's outline. Print it either way:

  • At home: print the saved PDF from your printer app, or with AirPrint.
  • At a convenience store: send the saved PDF to the store's multi-copy printer using its usual app or method, then print it there.

Important: when printing, always choose "actual size" / "100%" / "no scaling." Options like "fit to page" or "shrink to fit" will change the size of the outline and the four corner markers, and the App will not be able to read the sheet correctly afterward.

Does it work with a black-and-white printer?

Yes. The outline is a black-and-white line drawing, so any black-and-white printer (including convenience-store printers) works fine. Your child colors it in with whatever colors they like.

Tips for a good scan

  • Lay the paper flat: a curled or folded sheet cannot be read. Put it on a table or another flat surface.
  • Keep all four corner markers visible: the sheet has a marker in each corner. All four must be clearly visible on screen — if even one is hidden, scanning will not work.
  • Use a bright location: colors will not be read correctly in a dim room. Scan somewhere well lit.
  • Hold the phone still, straight above the paper: try to avoid camera shake, and hold the device directly above the sheet.
  • Frame only the paper: if the desk or other objects are visible in the frame, scanning may fail. Get close enough that only the paper fills the screen.
  • If a face appears in the camera view, that moment is skipped and nothing is scanned — make sure only the paper is in frame.

What's free and what requires a purchase

Free, no purchase needed:

  • Coloring a car (on screen or on printed paper) — unlimited
  • Making and keeping up to 2 cars
  • Racing on 1 track, up to 3 races total

With a purchase:

  • Racing beyond the first 3 free races
  • All remaining cars and tracks are unlocked

See the Terms of Use for prices and plan details.

How to restore purchases

After a new device or reinstalling the App, open the pricing screen (past the parental gate) and tap "Restore Purchases." If you are signed in with the same Apple Account used to buy it, your purchase will be restored.

How to cancel a subscription

Open the Settings app → tap your name (Apple Account) → Subscriptions, and cancel at any time. Access continues until the end of the period already paid for. The Lifetime plan does not auto-renew, so there is nothing to cancel.

How to delete your data

In the App, open the "Parents' Area" (a short parental gate is shown first) and choose "Delete all data." This clears everything the App has stored, including any cars your child made, returning the App to its first-launch state. Deleting the App itself also removes its data from the device.
