Nurie Racer

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026 · Developer: Bruce Chu (independent developer)

This page explains how the iOS app "Nurie Racer" (the "App") handles information about your child and you as a parent or guardian. It is written for parents.

Summary: The App does not require an account. The developer does not collect any personal information. Camera images are processed entirely on the device and are never sent to any server. The App does not include any third-party analytics or advertising.

1. Information we collect

The developer (Bruce Chu) does not collect any personal information such as name, email address, location, or contacts. There is no account system and no login.

Children cannot type free text anywhere in the App, and cannot see text entered by other children. This prevents accidental sharing of personal information and inappropriate messages between children.

2. Camera use

The App may ask for camera access so your child can turn a printed coloring sheet into the paint job on their 3D car. The camera is used for exactly one purpose:

  • To photograph the printed coloring sheet and convert its colors into a car design, on the device.

Camera frames are processed entirely on the device. The video/image frames captured while scanning are never uploaded or sent to the developer's servers.

The only thing saved on the device after scanning is the finished car body texture (a color image). The raw camera frames captured during scanning are not saved; they are discarded on the device once processing is finished.

If a face appears in the frame: a frame that is detected to contain a human face is discarded immediately and is never used to build the car design, saved, or sent anywhere.

If camera permission is not granted, your child can still create a car by finger-painting directly on the screen.

3. What is stored, and where

The App stores only the following on your device (in Application Support storage, not uploaded anywhere):

  • The finished look (texture image) of each car your child made, its nickname, and the creation time.
  • The number of free races remaining (kept so that reinstalling the App cannot be used to reset it).
  • Purchase status (whether a subscription or the one-time purchase is active), verified on-device through Apple's StoreKit.
  • Settings such as sound, vibration, and steering mode.

4. Third parties

The App does none of the following:

  • Show advertising or connect to any ad network.
  • Include any third-party analytics or tracking SDK.
  • Automatically send content your child creates (car images, nicknames) anywhere outside the device.
  • Sell or share personal information with third parties.

The only network connection the App makes is to Apple's own servers (App Store / StoreKit) to process purchases. That purchase flow is handled by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy; the developer never receives payment details such as card numbers.

5. In-app purchases

All in-app purchases (subscriptions and the one-time purchase) are sold, billed, and managed entirely through Apple's App Store. See the Terms of Use for details.

6. How to delete your data

You can delete everything the App has stored at any time:

  1. In the App: open the "Parents' Area" (a short parental gate is shown first) and choose "Delete all data." This removes everything the App has saved on the device, including your child's created cars.
  2. By deleting the App: removing the App from your device deletes its stored data as well.

The count of remaining free races is kept separately to prevent abuse by reinstalling the App, so it is not affected by "Delete all data."

7. Children's privacy (for parents)

The App is listed in Apple's Kids Category (age range 6–8). This section is written for parents and guardians.

  • The App does not collect personal information from any user, including children.
  • The App does not include third-party advertising, behavioral/targeted ads, or third-party analytics.
  • Children cannot type free text or contact other users.
  • A short parental gate (a simple task a young child cannot easily solve) is shown before any purchase screen, external link (such as this Privacy Policy or the Terms of Use), printing, sharing, or system Settings screen.

Questions from parents are welcome at the contact address below.

8. Future features

Version 1.0 does not upload photos or anything your child creates to the internet. If a future version adds a feature to share cars with others online, this Privacy Policy will be updated before that feature is turned on, describing exactly what would be sent and when. Any such sharing is planned to be off by default and only happen when a parent explicitly turns it on.
